For the user's Instagram influencer sourcing (see [[user-role]]), the **most efficient method by far is following IG's "related/suggested accounts" recommendations** starting from an already-found suitable influencer (relationship-chain / 滚雪球). 

**Why:** Validated over several days of actual work — on 2026-05-28 morning the user found 6 suitable influencers in ~30 min this way. It has consistently been the highest-yield method. The "find endorsement influencers from brand videos/tagged posts" method (品牌视频/代言反查) was tried but is noticeably **less efficient**.

**How to apply:** When planning the user's sourcing work or suggesting methods, lead with the related-recommendations/snowball approach. Don't over-emphasize the brand-video reverse-lookup method — it underperforms for them. Keep at least one validated suitable influencer on hand each day as the seed for the next day's snowballing.
